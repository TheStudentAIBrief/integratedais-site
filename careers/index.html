Careers

Work on the AI integrations that regulated and sovereign organisations depend on

We're a small, senior team helping organisations adopt AI properly: from strategy through to secure, on-premises and air-gapped deployment. That means the people we hire need to be comfortable with rigour, with security constraints, and with clients who cannot afford to get this wrong.

How we work

Our positioning is deliberately broad — we don't chase a single sector or a single type of engagement — but the way we work is consistent across all of it. A few things matter more here than they might elsewhere.

Rigour first

We favour evidence over hype. Every recommendation, deployment and line of code is expected to hold up under scrutiny — from regulators, security teams and our own engineers.

Security-mindedness by default

We work with organisations that operate under strict security and compliance obligations, often deploying on-premises or fully air-gapped. Security is a design constraint from day one, not a bolt-on.

Direct client relationships

You'll work closely with the people who actually run the systems we integrate with, not several removes away through account layers. Expect direct exposure to real infrastructure and real constraints.

Broad technical range

Our work spans AI integration and adoption strategy, secure deployment architecture, data and MLOps, and ongoing managed support. We look for people comfortable moving across that range, not narrowly specialised.

Ownership over process

We're a small, senior team. You'll own outcomes end-to-end rather than a slice of a larger process, and we'd rather you flag a problem early than wait for a meeting to raise it.

Calm under regulated pressure

Working with regulated and sovereign clients means higher stakes and slower-moving constraints than a typical SaaS environment. We value people who stay methodical rather than move fast and break things.

Open roles

We don't have open roles listed right now. Opportunities connected to the launch of Integrated AI Academy will be posted here first, ahead of any external job board.

Speculative applications

We hire deliberately rather than on a fixed schedule, so a live vacancy isn't a precondition for us wanting to hear from you. If you think you'd be a strong fit for the kind of work described above — AI integration and adoption, secure or on-premises deployment, data and MLOps, strategy and advisory, or managed support — send your CV and a short note about what you'd want to work on.
