What you'll be able to check off

Know exactly where your data goes — and prove it

The checks that separate "cloud AI with a privacy policy" from a deployment where your data never leaves your control. On-premises, self-hosted, and hybrid options laid out against the questions your auditors will actually ask.

Score how a vendor handles being wrong

AI that's confident and wrong is the real risk in a regulated setting. Inside: the criteria for evaluating review loops, cross-checking, and human oversight — so you're buying evidence, not optimism.

Turn "we should have a policy" into a checklist

The governance, accountability, and audit-trail questions that turn an AI project from a board liability into a board-ready case — without needing to become an AI expert first.

Ask the questions that reveal lock-in before you sign

Independence, hand-over, and exit criteria: how to tell whether a partner is building capability into your team or building dependence on theirs. The questions most buyers only think to ask a year too late.

Straight answers before you hand over your email

Is this just a lead-gen form with a thin PDF behind it?

No. It's the actual assessment we run before we'll agree to build anything — 25 questions we hold our own work to. If it surfaces nothing you didn't already know, you've lost an email address and five minutes.

Who is it for?

Security, risk, and technology leaders in regulated or security-conscious organisations who are moving AI from pilot toward production and need it to survive a board, an auditor, and a red-team.

What do you do with my email?

We send you the checklist — that's it, unless you tick the box for occasional briefings. We don't share or sell your details. Delivery is transactional; marketing is separate and opt-in.

Do you resell AI vendors or push a product?

No. We're independent and vendor-neutral, so the assessment names the hard trade-offs instead of steering you toward something we'd earn commission on.

What happens after I score myself?

If it flags gaps you can't close on your own, there's a small, low-risk next step — but nothing is pitched on this page. Score it first; decide later.
